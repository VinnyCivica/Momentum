Demo script v6b: Draft care plan assistant (about 2.5 minutes)

The short version of the v6 script, with six cuts and tighter wording. For a Teams screen share to a panel. The point to land: the assistant does the reading in seconds, every suggestion shows where it came from, every decision is acknowledged for the audit trail, and nothing is written until a clinician signs off.

  • Say = what you say. Press = what you click. Do the clicks while you talk.
  • The narrated version, civica-care-plan-assistant-v6b-demo.htm, speaks these same lines and does these same clicks.
  • Length: about 330 words. At a normal pace (about 150 words a minute) plus the clicks, it runs about 2 min 35 s; the narrated version is about 2 min 25 s. If you need to save a few more seconds, speak the “Review” section a little faster.
  • Cut from v6: dismissing Mood and engagement; the Add a focus area panel (now one sentence); the source record from the “Why the assistant suggests this” tab; the Edit side panel (now one sentence); the Worth your attention description; the side menu and Care plan list ending.

Before you share (not timed)

  1. Open civica-care-plan-assistant-v6.0.htm in Edge or Chrome. Close other tabs and turn on Do not disturb.
  2. Leave the browser zoom at 100%, then check readability. Share the window in a Teams test call (or join from a second device) and make sure the chips and the “Worth your attention” text are easy to read. Only if they aren’t, zoom to 110–125%. Zooming can make the side menu open over the page instead of beside it, so rehearse at the zoom you’ll use.
  3. Press F5 to reset, then click Draft care plan in the sidebar (under Care plan). You should see Draft care plan: Summarize.
  4. In Teams, choose Share > Window and pick the browser window.

1 · Opening (0:00 – 0:15)

Say:

“This is a presentation prototype of the Draft care plan assistant in Civica Momentum, built with the latest Firefly design system and test data.”

Press: the side menu button (top left, next to the breadcrumb).

Say: “I’ll close the side menu for more space.”

2 · The resident and safety alerts (0:15 – 0:30)

Say:

“At the top are Peggy’s safety alerts, kept on every page so critical risks, like her penicillin allergy, are always in view.”

Press: Penicillin allergy (the first alert). A dialog opens.

Say: “Selecting an alert opens the record behind it.” Press: Close (the X).

3 · Summarize (0:30 – 0:55)

Say: “Now I’ll ask the assistant to summarize her record.”

Press: Summarize record. It takes about 4 seconds.

Say (while it runs): “It reads twelve parts of her record, from assessments to medications.”

When it finishes, say:

“In a few seconds it has read 101 records and drafted five focus areas. Without the assistant, a nurse would open each of the twelve parts on its own screen and piece the plan together themselves.”

4 · Worth your attention and Add a source (0:55 – 1:15)

Say:

“Every finding must be acknowledged, checked now or follow up later, for the audit trail.”

Press, top to bottom in Worth your attention: Checked now, Checked now, Follow up later (Mood signals), Checked now.

Press: Add a source (beside Sources read). A side panel opens.

Say:

“Missing information can be added as a source in a side panel, such as a hospital discharge letter.”

Press: Cancel in the panel.

Say: “Now let’s review the draft care plan.” Press: Review the draft care plan.

5 · Review (1:15 – 2:05)

Say: “On the left are the five focus areas.”

Point to: Falls prevention on the right.

Say:

“Falls prevention shows its sources and a point to check: the alert says walker, but her last fall was from a wheelchair.”

Say:

“The nurse reads both tabs before deciding. The ‘Why the assistant suggests this’ tab lists the evidence, each piece linked to its source record.”

Press: the Outcomes (2) and interventions (4) tab.

Say: “The second tab holds the outcomes and interventions, which can be edited.”

Say:

“Every selection is acknowledged with a tick box, which is important for the audit trail.”

Press:

  1. Tick I have checked the outcomes and interventions.
  2. Checked now on the point to check.
  3. Accept focus area.

Say: “The other areas are reviewed the same way. Nurses can also add their own.”

Press, for each of the next four areas: › (Next focus area), tick the confirmation, Checked now if there’s a point to check, then Accept focus area.

6 · Sign off (2:05 – 2:15)

Press: Continue to sign off.

Say:

“Sign off shows what will change and what is still open. Nothing changes until a clinician signs off. The nurse ticks the declaration and takes clinical responsibility.”

Press: tick the declaration, then Sign off and activate.

7 · Result and close (2:15 – 2:35)

Say: “Every step is in the audit trail, including who signed off.”

Press: View care plan.

Say:

“And here is her new active care plan. The assistant does the reading in seconds. Every suggestion is traceable, every decision is the nurse’s, and the audit trail is complete. That’s time given back to resident care.”

Stop sharing.

If something goes wrong

  • A red “There is a problem” box: a tick or radio button was missed. Click the link in the box; it jumps to the missing item.
  • Continue to sign off doesn’t move on: one focus area isn’t decided yet. Look for one in the left list without Accepted.
  • To start again: press F5, then click Draft care plan.
